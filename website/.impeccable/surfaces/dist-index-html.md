## Direction contract

THESIS: Bring the remastered game box to life as a project exhibit. The fiery
cover leads; real screenshots and restored maps carry the rest.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black, box-art yellow and flame orange, followed by the ivory
and olive of the approved mission maps. Barlow Condensed supplies strong upright
titles. Controls are crisp rectangles, with no dashboard cards or military UI costume.

STORY: Recognise Dynamix's game, inspect the restoration, browse its missions,
and understand how to install with a separately supplied PC copy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A tall intact cover occupies the right half. An oversized cream
M1 Abrams title and orange Fan Remaster occupy the left, with a short description,
original creator credit and a visible installation action. Mobile preserves the
cover and makes navigation wrap without a hidden-menu dependency.

FORM: Supplied-art exhibit, code-led provisional implementation. No random seed
or concept tournament was used, preserving the already approved visual authority.
Signature interaction: same-frame EGA, Genesis and Upscaled comparison. Motion is
one subtle cover settle; all content is visible by default, reduced motion wins.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Working if: a visitor can identify the game, operate both galleries, find the
required-PC installation information and original creators at desktop and mobile.
