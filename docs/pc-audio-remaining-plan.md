## Completion criteria

1. Every confirmed game sound family has a selected, honestly labelled source,
   or an explicit original-game absence, approved fallback or acquisition hold.
2. A recording is clean enough after actual audition, with no stray speech,
   music, extra shots, obvious looping, unwanted passby or onboard perspective.
3. Each binding has a demonstrated original action or currently visible source
   owner. No guessed request semantics, hidden cues or manufactured guest state.
4. Original event timing, cadence, masks, motor authority, voices and accepted
   balances survive. Independent Off and saved gain remain usable.
5. Originals, edits, provenance, licence and rollback stay recoverable. Actual
   export contents meet their intended-use and distribution terms.
6. The named target edition actually loads its intended sounds, with appropriate
   native/device and release checks. Source-only completion is labelled local.

Working if: each planned row ends with one of those evidenced dispositions,
and neither an acquired file nor a passing unit test is called a shipped sound.

## 5. Rights and release decisions

The current free T-80 source explicitly carries
[CC BY-NC4.0](https://freesound.org/people/SoundFX.studio/sounds/456277/).
For an asset-bearing edition, check its actual intended use and required credit
against those terms. Commercial use or unresolved compatibility requires a
cleared fallback or appropriate additional rights. No duplicate purchase is
needed for the accepted current private use. Paid sources remain outside
public Git; the [SoundFX Studio licence](https://soundfx.studio/soundfx-studio-license/)
permits game synchronisation/edits subject to its single-editor/local-storage
and standalone-sharing restrictions. Other vendors retain their own terms.

Do not assume payment alone clears loose raw-file publication, or that every
licensed game derivative must remain permanently unusable. Review the exact
intended edition, audience, asset packaging and extraction terms. Retain
attribution/modification notices and proof of purchase. No new raw audio goes
to models. A source commit or browser-shell update is not delivery of the
private recordings.
