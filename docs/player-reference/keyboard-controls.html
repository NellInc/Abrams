Quick save, slot 1Cmd+SCtrl+Alt+S

Session provides five save slots, Undo last load and Normal, 2x, 4x or 8x fast forward. Graphics switches EGA, Genesis, Upscaled and Modern. Audio is muted during fast forward.

Pause with Esc before opening a reference if you want the original simulation to wait. Help never sends an extra command to the game. Release held keys after returning from a menu or another window.

Stations and general commands

KeyAction
QOpen quit sequence for scenario or campaign; continuation option cancels quit.
F1Select gunner station.
F2Select tank commander station.
F3Select cupola view.
F4Select driver station.
F5Toggle sound.
Shift+3Change system speed among slow, medium and fast; fast is the load default.
EscBack out of menu selection; during a scenario pause, then press any key to resume.

Hull and turret

KeyAction
AAlign turret with the front of the hull.
CSwitch movement control between hull and turret.
Keypad 2 / DownMove backward in hull mode; lower sight in turret mode.
Keypad 4 / LeftTurn hull or turret left; decrease selected ammunition mix in Motor Pool.
Keypad 5Stop hull or turret movement.
Keypad 6 / RightTurn hull or turret right; increase selected ammunition mix in Motor Pool.
Keypad 8 / UpMove forward in hull mode; raise sight in turret mode.

Gunner

KeyAction
LLock crosshairs onto the currently selected target.
MFire machine gun.
SDischarge smoke canisters.
1Ready HEAT.
2Ready sabot.
3Ready AX.
EnterCycle available targets with the TADS box; after the final target the box turns off.
SpaceFire selected weapon.
ZCycle normal 1x, 3x and 10x sight magnification.

Commander

KeyAction
ZAlternate between full territory map and local close-up.
DShow system damage screen; any key returns to commander station.
F7Return scan to original/current turret bearing.
F8Scan 90 degrees clockwise from turret bearing.
F9Scan 180 degrees from turret bearing.
F10Scan 270 degrees clockwise from turret bearing.

Radio and thermal

KeyAction
RRetrieve pending radio message; no effect when no message is waiting.
TToggle thermal imaging.

Menus and briefings

KeyAction
EnterCycle highlighted mission, DAY/NIGHT or NOVICE/MODERATE/EXPERT options; submit campaign name or vehicle identification.
SpaceAdvance past mission-title screen.
EnterAdvance timed presentation screens such as credits or Colonel Wilson briefing.

Original controls: manual pp. 4 to 7, with station and menu context on pp. 9 to 20. Remaster shortcuts are taken from the implemented Play controls.
